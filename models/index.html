01 — ABOUT MINDBRIDGE

What is MindBridge?

MindBridge is a browser-based, zero-server mental wellness intelligence platform designed to detect, quantify, and track psychological distress using multi-modal AI inference. Built on peer-reviewed research in affective computing, clinical psychology, and signal processing, it democratizes access to mental health screening tools previously available only in clinical settings.

Designed for individuals, wellness circles, college counselors, and researchers, MindBridge processes text and vocal inputs entirely on-device — your entries are not uploaded anywhere. Your text and voice are analysed on your device; the AI model files are downloaded once from a public CDN.

02 — CLINICAL PIPELINE

How MindBridge Processes Your Data

A 5-stage multi-modal inference pipeline converts raw user input into a clinically-informed composite risk score — entirely on-device.

01
📝

Data Acquisition

Text input (free-form journal, Hindi/English) and/or vocal audio via WebAudio API MediaRecorder. Optional PHQ-9 / GAD-7 structured questionnaire responses.

02
🔤

NLP Text Analysis

Lexicon and phrase-pattern matching (with negation handling) on a bilingual Hindi-English clinical dictionary. Computes length-normalised risk scores, identifies trigger and protective language patterns. Score normalized to 0–100.

Lexicon NLPNegationRisk Scoring

03
🧠

Emotion AI Inference

A DistilRoBERTa transformer (Transformers.js, ONNX runtime) classifies text into emotions (Sadness, Joy, Anger, Fear, Surprise; Love when the model supports it). Falls back to NLP-inferred profile if model loading.

DistilRoBERTaONNX6-class
→
04
🎙️

Vocal Biomarker Analysis

Prosodic feature extraction from raw microphone audio: fundamental frequency (F0, autocorrelation), frame-level jitter and shimmer, speech rate, energy and pause ratio. Experimental: measured with a browser microphone, so values are not comparable to lab (Praat) measurements and are not clinically validated.

F0/JitterShimmerProsody
→
05
📊

Composite Score Fusion

Weighted fusion: Score = 0.4×Text + 0.3×Voice + 0.3×Face, where Text = 0.5×NLP lexicon + 0.5×Emotion model (weights are hand-chosen, not fitted to data). Maps to 4-level clinical risk: Low / Moderate / High / Severe with actionable recommendations and helpline escalation.

Weighted FusionRisk BandsEscalation
Explainability is implemented via a LIME-style word saliency map — each word's contribution to the risk score is highlighted in the report view, enabling transparent, interpretable AI decisions.

06 — ETHICS & RESEARCH IMPACT

Built for India. Built on Science.

India faces a mental health treatment gap of over 83% — fewer than 1 in 6 people with mental illness receive treatment. MindBridge addresses this through three principles:

01
Accessibility — Works on any device with a browser. No app install. No data plan concern. Fully offline after first load.
02
Stigma Reduction — Private, anonymous analysis removes the barrier of social stigma that prevents millions from seeking help.
03
Evidence-Based — Every feature references peer-reviewed clinical literature. Word-level explanations show which words influenced the score.
🔎 What is running right now: checking emotion model…
Known limitations: fusion weights are hand-chosen, not fitted to data · voice and face measures are experimental (browser microphone / camera) · the word-list scoring is a heuristic, not the trained model from the paper · none of the composite scores have been validated in a clinical study.
⚠️ MindBridge is a screening and support tool, not a clinical diagnostic instrument. PHQ-9 and GAD-7 results should be reviewed by a qualified mental health professional. If you are in crisis, use the 🆘 Help Now button immediately.

About MindBridge

Web implementation & extension of a research paper accepted at IC3-2026 — 18th IEEE International Conference on Contemporary Computing · JIIT Noida & University of Florida · Aug 6–8, 2026 🇮🇳

📄
✅ Accepted — IC3-2026 · IEEE · Aug 6–8, 2026

Explainable AI For Early Prediction of Anxiety and Depression in India: A Dual-Modality Framework

Srimant Bhardwaj & Dr. Navneeta Bharadvaja · Dept. of Biotechnology, Delhi Technological University

Accepted for presentation and publication at the 18th IEEE-sponsored International Conference on Contemporary Computing (IC3-2026), organized by Jaypee Institute of Information Technology (JIIT), Noida & University of Florida, USA — Hybrid format, Aug 6–8, 2026 ✨

The paper proposes a two-phase XAI pipeline: Phase 1 uses XGBoost + SMOTE on structured lifestyle & socio-demographic data (1,200 records, 79.95% accuracy). Phase 2 uses TF-IDF + XGBoost on 53,000+ unstructured text statements (93.73% accuracy, 96.10% precision). SHAP provides global feature importance; LIME provides instance-level word attribution. MindBridge is the browser-based implementation and extension of this work.

XGBoost + SMOTE TF-IDF NLP · 93.73% SHAP + LIME India-Specific ✅ IC3-2026 · IEEE · JIIT & UF
⚠️ Scope boundary: The underlying research paper has been accepted at IC3-2026 (18th IEEE International Conference on Contemporary Computing). The trained XGBoost models are not part of this browser app (an optional Python backend exists but is not deployed here). This browser app uses research-inspired heuristics that conceptually approximate the paper's methodology — not the exact trained models. PHQ-9 & GAD-7 remain clinically validated instruments.
🧬

NLP Text Analysis Paper Phase 2

Paper: TF-IDF vectorization on 53,000+ text statements → XGBoost classifier → 93.73% accuracy, 96.10% precision. MindBridge: Browser-side TF-IDF heuristic + 200+ word bilingual lexicon as an approximation. LIME word attribution implemented natively.

📊

Lifestyle Risk Analysis Paper Phase 1

Paper: XGBoost + SMOTE on 1,200 structured socio-demographic records (sleep, activity, stress, social support) → 79.95% accuracy. SHAP identified top risk features. MindBridge Tracker implements this as a live daily logging system.

🎙️

Vocal Biomarkers Paper Future Work

The paper explicitly listed vocal feature integration as future work. MindBridge implements this early: WebAudio API extracts F0, RMS energy, frame-level jitter & shimmer, speech rate and pause ratio in real time. This is an experimental browser-side extension of the paper's stated direction.

🤖

Emotion AI Extended

Not in the original paper. MindBridge adds a pre-trained transformer layer (DistilRoBERTa via Transformers.js) for multi-class emotion detection — a capability extension beyond the paper's TF-IDF binary classification approach.

📊 Paper → Web App: What Maps to What

Phase 1
Structured Lifestyle Risk Analysis — 79.95% accuracy

In paper: XGBoost + SMOTE on 1,200 records of sleep, physical activity, financial stress & social support. SHAP reveals top risk predictors globally.
In MindBridge: The Lifestyle Tracker implements this as a live daily logging system — same variables, user-driven input.

Phase 2
Behavioral Text Analysis (NLP) — 93.73% accuracy · 96.10% precision

In paper: TF-IDF vectorization on 53,000+ unstructured text statements → XGBoost classifier. LIME highlights word-level risk contributors.
In MindBridge: Browser TF-IDF heuristic + 200+ word bilingual lexicon approximates this. LIME-style word highlighting implemented natively in the Report view.

Future Work
Vocal Biomarker Integration — Implemented early in MindBridge

In paper: Explicitly listed as future work — "incorporating vocal features to better assess the emotions driven for anxiety and depression."
In MindBridge: Real-time WebAudio API extracts F0, RMS energy, frame-level jitter & shimmer, speech rate and pause ratio in-browser. Experimental early implementation of the paper's stated future direction.

Extended
New Additions Beyond the Paper's Scope

transformer emotion AI (deep learning emotion layer), PHQ-9 + GAD-7 clinically validated screening, bilingual Hindi-English support, private journal, CBT breathing & grounding exercises, 30-day mood tracker, crisis helplines for India, and a fully offline PWA.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

MindBridge is an educational and supportive tool. The PHQ-9 and GAD-7 are clinically validated clinical instruments — their scores carry scientific meaning. The composite AI risk score (combining text, voice and facial signals) is a research-inspired heuristic with custom-weighted parameters and has not been externally validated in a clinical study. It is indicative only and should never replace consultation with a licensed psychiatrist or mental health professional. If you are in crisis, please contact a helpline immediately.

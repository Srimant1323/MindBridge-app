01 — ABOUT MINDBRIDGE

What is MindBridge?

MindBridge is a browser-based, zero-server mental wellness intelligence platform designed to detect, quantify, and track psychological distress using multi-modal AI inference. Built on peer-reviewed research in affective computing, clinical psychology, and signal processing, it democratizes access to mental health screening tools previously available only in clinical settings.

Designed for individuals, wellness circles, college counselors, and researchers, MindBridge processes text and vocal inputs entirely on-device — your entries are not uploaded anywhere. Your text and voice are analysed on your device; the AI model files are downloaded once from a public CDN.

02 — CLINICAL PIPELINE

How MindBridge Processes Your Data

A 5-stage multi-modal inference pipeline converts raw user input into a clinically-informed composite risk score — entirely on-device.

01
📝

Data Acquisition

Text input (free-form journal, Hindi/English) and/or vocal audio via WebAudio API MediaRecorder. Optional PHQ-9 / GAD-7 structured questionnaire responses.

🔤

NLP Text Analysis

Lexicon and phrase-pattern matching (with negation handling) on a bilingual Hindi-English clinical dictionary. Computes length-normalised risk scores, identifies trigger and protective language patterns. Score normalized to 0–100.

Lexicon NLPNegationRisk Scoring

03
🧠

Emotion AI Inference

A DistilRoBERTa transformer (Transformers.js, ONNX runtime) classifies text into emotions (Sadness, Joy, Anger, Fear, Surprise; Love when the model supports it). Falls back to NLP-inferred profile if model loading.

DistilRoBERTaONNX6-class
→
04
🎙️

Vocal Biomarker Analysis

Prosodic feature extraction from raw microphone audio: fundamental frequency (F0, autocorrelation), frame-level jitter and shimmer, speech rate, energy and pause ratio. Experimental: measured with a browser microphone, so values are not comparable to lab (Praat) measurements and are not clinically validated.

F0/JitterShimmerProsody
→
05
📊

Composite Score Fusion

Weighted fusion: Score = 0.4×Text + 0.3×Voice + 0.3×Face, where Text = 0.5×NLP lexicon + 0.5×Emotion model (weights are hand-chosen, not fitted to data). Maps to 4-level clinical risk: Low / Moderate / High / Severe with actionable recommendations and helpline escalation.

Weighted FusionRisk BandsEscalation
Explainability is implemented via a LIME-style word saliency map — each word's contribution to the risk score is highlighted in the report view, enabling transparent, interpretable AI decisions.

06 — ETHICS & RESEARCH IMPACT

Built for India. Built on Science.

India faces a mental health treatment gap of over 83% — fewer than 1 in 6 people with mental illness receive treatment. MindBridge addresses this through three principles:

01
Accessibility — Works on any device with a browser. No app install. No data plan concern. Fully offline after first load.
02
Stigma Reduction — Private, anonymous analysis removes the barrier of social stigma that prevents millions from seeking help.
03
Evidence-Based — Every feature references peer-reviewed clinical literature. Word-level explanations show which words influenced the score.
🔎 What is running right now: checking emotion model…
Known limitations: fusion weights are hand-chosen, not fitted to data · voice and face measures are experimental (browser microphone / camera) · the word-list scoring is a heuristic, not the trained model from the paper · none of the composite scores have been validated in a clinical study.
⚠️ MindBridge is a screening and support tool, not a clinical diagnostic instrument. PHQ-9 and GAD-7 results should be reviewed by a qualified mental health professional. If you are in crisis, use the 🆘 Help Now button immediately.
